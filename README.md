# ma-lit-bills

A neutral tracker of Massachusetts bills that touch technology: AI, privacy and data protection, cybersecurity and breach notice, biometrics, kids' online safety, digital right-to-repair, broadband, autonomous vehicles, crypto and fintech, and court technology.

For each bill: status, committee, hearing dates, sponsors, topic tags, and a short plain-language summary written or checked by a person. A weekly digest feeds the LITSA newsletter, and the tracker will appear at suffolklitsa.org/bills.

**Neutral by design.** The tracker describes bills; it does not support or oppose them.

Later: court decisions interpreting the statutes once enacted.
